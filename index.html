New game

Clears your research, cities and tracker. Your tribe is kept.

How to use

  • Swipe left and right, or tap the tabs at the bottom, to change page.
  • Research: tap a skill to buy it. Its stars come off, it gets a ring in your colour and it opens the outer ring.
  • Buildings and Units show what your research has unlocked. Tap Build or Train to pay for one.
  • Your stars are always shown on the banner at the top.
  • Ruins: roll the die when you explore a ruin.
  • Tracker: your stars, stars per go and super unit health.
